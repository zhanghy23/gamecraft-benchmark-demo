LOST

You wake up on a mysterious island after a shipwreck. The radio transmitter was scattered into three parts across the island: the transmitter, antenna, and battery.

Search the terrain for food to survive and collect all three parts to send a distress signal. Find shelter before starvation takes you.

Controls:
Arrow Keys - Move
Space - Pick up items

Press SPACE to start

Game Over

You died from starvation. No one ever found you. You are lost forever.

Press SPACE to try again

Rescued

A fishing boat got your call and is on its way to get you. Seems you are not lost after all :).

Press SPACE to play again
