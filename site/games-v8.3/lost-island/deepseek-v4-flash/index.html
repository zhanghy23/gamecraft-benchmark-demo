Lost

You wake up on a deserted island with no memory of how you got here. The only thing you know is that you need to find food to survive and locate three radio parts to call for rescue.

Use the arrow keys to move around. Press SPACE to interact with nearby objects.

Press space to start

Game Over

You died from starvation. No one ever found you. You are lost forever.

Rescued

A fishing boat got your call and is on its way to get you. Seems you are not lost after all :).
