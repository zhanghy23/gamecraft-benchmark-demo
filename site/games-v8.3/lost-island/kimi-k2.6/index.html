Lost

You wake up on a mysterious island with no memory of how you got there. The waves crash against the shore as you struggle to your feet. You must find food to survive and gather the parts to build a rescue radio. Three pieces are scattered across the island: a transmitter, an antenna, and a battery. Find them all to call for help before starvation takes you.
WASD or Arrow Keys to move
Space to interact / pick up items
Press space to start

Game Over

You died from starvation. No one ever found you. You are lost forever.

Rescued

A fishing boat get your call and is on its way to get you. Seems you are not lost after all :).
