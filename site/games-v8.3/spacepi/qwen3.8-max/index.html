SpacePi

Overview

Objective: Protect your growing circular base from incoming enemy lines by drawing defensive barriers. Survive until the base reaches its goal size to complete each level.

Controls: Click and drag to draw defensive lines (costs power). Press Space to pause. Move your cursor near coins and powerups to collect them.
